# Retail Sales & Customer Analytics

## Key Business Insights

1. **Regional Gap**: West leads with 14.94% profit margin but 
   Central generates $501K revenue at only 7.92% margin — 
   heavy discounting likely causing profit leakage.

2. **Loss-Making Products**: 2 of the top 10 products by sales 
   are unprofitable (Cisco unit: -$1,811, Binding System: -$1,878). 
   High sales volume masking actual losses.

3. **Best Margin Products**: Paper (43%) and Copiers (37%) are 
   the most margin-efficient sub-categories — underutilized 
   relative to their profitability.

4. **Furniture Problem**: Chairs is the top Furniture product 
   at $328K sales but only 8.1% margin — volume without value.

5. **Hidden Gem Segment**: Home Office customers have highest 
   margin (14.03%) and AOV ($240) but only 148 customers — 
   biggest growth opportunity.

6. **Peak Season Paradox**: November is highest sales month 
   ($118K) but lowest margin (8.2%) — discounting during 
   peak season is destroying profit when it matters most.
